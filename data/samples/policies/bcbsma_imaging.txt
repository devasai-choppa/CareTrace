Advanced Imaging Policy IMG-2
Payer: Blue Cross Blue Shield of Massachusetts
Effective date: 2026-01-15

## Prior authorization
Outpatient elective knee MRI requires prior authorization before the service is scheduled.
Emergency imaging rendered in the emergency department does not require prior authorization.

## Documentation
Submit clinical notes and a record of conservative therapy for six weeks.
